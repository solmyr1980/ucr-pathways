What would you like to study?

Explore how your interests could fit together in a three-year Liberal Arts and Sciences degree at University College Roosevelt.

Browse study options

  • AI, language and the brain

    Explore how people perceive, think and understand language. Study those questions alongside programming, neural networks and the philosophy of mind.

  • Biomedical science and health

    Trace how cells and organs work, what changes in disease and how researchers investigate health in the laboratory and across populations.

  • Earth, climate and economics

    Connect climate change, water and natural resources with economics. Study how land is used, how communities adapt and how sustainability decisions are made.

  • Economics, business and data

    Study markets and business decisions, then use econometrics, market research and machine learning to investigate them.

  • Environmental modelling and data science

    Study floods, soils and water alongside Earth observation and spatial analysis. Build skills in programming, machine learning and neural networks.

  • Film, literature and visual culture

    Read novels alongside their screen adaptations, study documentary film and explore how writers and filmmakers tell stories across cultures.

  • Politics, law and public institutions

    Compare how democracies govern, how courts interpret rights and how governments decide where public money goes.
