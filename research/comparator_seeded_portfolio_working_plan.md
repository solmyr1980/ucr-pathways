# Comparator-seeded synthetic portfolio — working plan

**Status:** Working research record, not an authoritative production specification.

This file preserves the current experimental workflow so the work can proceed one step at a time without reconstructing prior decisions. Durable decisions should be moved into the authoritative UCR Pathways documentation only after the experiment is evaluated and approved.

## Current experiment

Instead of generating synthetic prospective-student interests first and then searching for a disciplinary comparator, test the reverse approach:

1. start from current Dutch research-university bachelor programmes;
2. use each programme's own student-facing language to identify the interests or questions it appeals to;
3. preserve that comparator-derived interest language for the pilot rather than broadening it in advance;
4. test whether UCR can construct credible alternative pathways;
5. evaluate the resulting comparisons before deciding whether the method should become permanent.

The current ten-programme pilot seeds are:

1. Psychobiologie — University of Amsterdam
2. Environmental Sciences — Wageningen University & Research
3. Informatica — Utrecht University
4. Health Sciences — Maastricht University
5. Kunstgeschiedenis — Utrecht University
6. Rechtsgeleerdheid — Utrecht University
7. Biologie — Utrecht University
8. Business Administration — University of Amsterdam
9. Psychology — Tilburg University
10. Communicatiewetenschap — University of Amsterdam

## Proposed 100-programme workflow

Work one step at a time and save the output of each step before continuing.

### Step 1 — Build an external longlist of Dutch WO bachelor programmes

Goal: create a broad, UCR-independent candidate universe before any UCR feasibility filtering.

Primary discovery source: Studiekeuze123, because it provides a current browsable overview of recognised Dutch WO bachelor offerings and useful metadata for programme, institution, city, study form and subject area.

Official status check: DUO RIO / Erkenningenoverzicht HO, because RIO is the official register of accredited higher-education programmes.

Procedure:

1. Capture all current Dutch **WO bachelor** offerings from Studiekeuze123.
2. Keep offerings that have a **full-time** route; include programmes that are offered both full-time and part-time, but exclude part-time-only offerings for this prospective-undergraduate comparison exercise.
3. Do not use the UCR course database at this stage.
4. Preserve the raw offering-level data first rather than manually selecting attractive subjects.
5. Store at minimum: programme name, institution, city/location, study form, broad Studiekeuze123 subject area, numerus-fixus indicator where available, source URL or identifier, and a current-status verification field.
6. Cross-check active/accredited status against DUO RIO / Erkenningenoverzicht HO and retain the official programme code where available.
7. Create a second, deduplicated candidate view at **programme + institution** level. Multiple locations or study forms from the same institution should normally collapse to one candidate unless the curriculum is materially different.
8. Mark the ten existing pilot seeds in the longlist; do not otherwise privilege them.
9. Do not yet exclude candidates because they look difficult for UCR. That belongs to Step 2.
10. Do not yet score source quality, student-interest language or distinctiveness. Those belong to later steps.

Likely scale: Studiekeuze123 currently exposes roughly 447–448 WO bachelor offerings before these filters and deduplication. The expected programme+institution longlist after removing part-time-only duplicates and collapsing duplicate offerings should be substantially smaller and suitable as the input to Step 2.
