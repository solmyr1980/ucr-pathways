# Expansion review

The second phase adds 16 programmes across six fields. The dataset now covers 40 programmes at 12 universities, with 265 structured criteria and 56 official source records. The additional candidates were screened against the same UCR workbook and six-term planning window as the pilot.

| Outcome | Original pilot | Added | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A. Published academic requirements appear satisfiable | 10 | 10 | 20 |
| B. Partial preparation with an identified academic gap | 5 | 0 | 5 |
| C. Eligibility unresolved | 9 | 6 | 15 |

The expansion deliberately favoured destinations with a substantive connection to UCR and routes that test different admissions rules. It gives no estimate of the share of Dutch master's programmes accessible to UCR graduates. Twenty-nine programmes in the combined sample had concrete academic criteria that could be structured. Eligibility still depends on the intake, transcript and university decision. [Programme records](programmes.csv) · [Criteria](admission_requirements.csv) · [Feasibility](feasibility.csv)

## Selection and curriculum checks

The added programmes cover public policy, European studies, international relations, psychology, quantitative methods, business, data science and computing, environmental sciences, anthropology and media. Broad matches on programme titles in the supplied alumni workbook supported this scope. Among 2,038 alumni rows with at least one parsed master's entry, the broad matches were business and management (194), psychology and methods (166), governance and international affairs (136), climate and marine sciences (74), media and anthropology (63), and data and computing (46). These are overlapping historical title matches across destinations. They are an indicator of subject relevance and do not measure current applicant demand or admissions rates. The repository records no individual alumni data.

Every candidate set fit the Fall 2026 to Spring 2029 horizon with formal prerequisites, four places per term and PPD in year one. This check establishes coexistence of the candidate courses. The six worked examples also passed the full 180 EC schedule check. The expanded dataset records 16 additional admissions assessments and candidate course sets.

Several routes give students an actionable course choice. Erasmus publishes an assessed public-policy route for relevant LAS graduates with at least 7.5 EC of WO research methods and a 7.0 GPA. Maastricht Responsible Data Science specifies a combined 30 EC pool spanning statistics, probability, linear algebra and programming. Utrecht's New Media & Digital Culture specifies separate pools of 15 EC of advanced media coursework and 7.5 EC of relevant methods. These rules can guide course selection before the institution evaluates the complete application. [Erasmus admissions](https://www.eur.nl/en/master/international-public-management-and-public-policy/admission) · [Maastricht admissions](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/education/master/programmes/responsible-data-science/admission-requirements) · [Utrecht admissions](https://www.uu.nl/en/masters/new-media-digital-culture/application-and-admission/degree-from-a-dutch-research-university)

## What the rechecks established

The nine original C cases still require an individual decision or further evidence. Two now have better documented alternatives. UvA publishes a 30 EC Media Studies pre-master for eligible academic bachelor's holders. Utrecht's Applied Data Science minor pages clarify how its courses are organised and how registration depends on student group. Both remain separate from direct-entry judgments on the UCR course plan. [UvA pre-master](https://www.uva.nl/shared-content/programmas/en/pre-masters-programmes/media-studies/media-studies.html) · [Utrecht minor](https://students.uu.nl/onderwijs/minors/applied-data-science)

Utrecht names a UCR route for Social, Health and Organisational Psychology using earlier course names. Its detailed criteria clarify the key research requirement. The applicant needs a quantitative thesis or equivalent report of at least 10 EC covering the full empirical cycle. Utrecht publicly describes a UCR Research Seminar leading to a thesis. Its availability and suitability for an individual quantitative project need verification. The existing fixed 24-course schedule validator cannot establish this. The same distinction applies to the written-thesis condition in the Radboud Biology example. [Psychology admission requirements](https://www.uu.nl/en/masters/social-health-and-organisational-psychology/application-and-admission/degree-from-a-dutch-research-university) · [Detailed norms](https://www.uu.nl/sites/default/files/SHOP%20-%20Entry%20requirements.pdf) · [UCR curriculum](https://www.uu.nl/en/bachelors/university-college-roosevelt/study-programme)
