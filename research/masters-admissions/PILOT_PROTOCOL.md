# Pilot protocol

## Sample and unit of analysis

Select four Dutch research-university master's programmes in each of six broad fields. Use the 21 August 2026 DUO/RIO export. Record the institution-specific programme unit, its recognised programme code (or parent code for a registered variant), the official programme name and the exact route. The counselor registry identifies bachelor's targets and its comparison IDs do not serve as master's identifiers. Include both regular and research degrees, with a deliberate range from interdisciplinary entry to named-degree and specialist requirements. Freeze the sample in the programme dataset and preserve unresolved results.

## Admission evidence

Use the official admissions route for a Dutch WO degree and research the September entry route unless stated otherwise. Separate current published criteria from explicitly dated regulations. If a page does not date its criteria, record the verification date and mark the cycle as unstated, even when the application timetable lists a year. When degree-based, content-based and bridging routes coexist, preserve them separately and specify which route governs the UCR assessment. Where only a cross-category or university-wide source is available, record its scope explicitly. Consult current formal regulations where a material conflict persists. Keep evidence statements short and paraphrased. Store a URL and section locator for each condition. Distinguish minimums from typical profiles, selection and recommendations. Silence about a threshold, exemption or bridge supplies no evidence of its existence or absence.

## UCR assessment

Use the current enriched workbook directly. Consider official course descriptions and the available profile, topics and methods from outlines. Assign credits only where evidence supports the required content and depth. Keep allocations to independent thresholds disjoint. A subminimum, cap or level condition inside an overall minimum constrains the same pool of credits. A mixed course contributes only where its scope supports the credit claim. Identify coursework gaps separately from uncertain equivalence, discretionary decisions and conditions that depend on individual applicants.

A indicates that the published academic criteria appear satisfiable. B indicates partial preparation with an identified material academic gap. C indicates that ambiguity, discretion or incomplete evidence prevents a defensible conclusion. A documented gap supports B even if other items remain unresolved. Discretion over the final admission decision is common and does not by itself preclude A when the published academic criteria are specific enough to assess.

## Examples and checks

Use Fall 2026 through Spring 2029 as a retrospective cohort planning illustration within the workbook's covered horizon. Retain the official offering flags and sequence all formal prerequisites earlier than their dependent courses. Use six consecutive terms, four distinct 7.5 EC courses per term, at least six 300-level courses and Personal & Professional Development once in year one. Reuse the shared course validator with temporary pilot inputs. Review academic progression and coherence separately. Any research proposal or writing sample remains conditional on the work the student completes and the receiving university's recognition of it.

Check the extraction model after the first field. Recheck route identity, source scope, quantitative thresholds and conditional logic against official text before finalising each field. Confirm dataset joins and sample counts. Check each example's allocations against its schedule and the course evidence. Record changes to the method and unresolved evidence in the pilot.
