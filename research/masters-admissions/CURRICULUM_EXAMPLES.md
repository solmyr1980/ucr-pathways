# Six UCR curriculum examples

Each example uses the Fall 2026 cohort's six published planning terms, four courses per term and 180 EC. Every course carries 7.5 EC. These are cohort illustrations, and the programmes reviewed here publish admissions information for earlier cycles than this cohort's expected master's entry. Check the actual master's requirements and UCR offering flags again before advising a student on a future start. The [flat schedules](curriculum_schedules.csv) include the official course codes and titles. The [requirements](admission_requirements.csv) and [sources](sources.csv) identify the official admissions evidence by route.

## MA-04 · Erasmus University Rotterdam · Social Inequalities and Change

**A · Published academic requirements appear satisfiable.** The assessed Sociology route accepts a comparable degree related to sociology, expressly including Liberal Arts and Sciences where the transcript supports the relationship. It expects sociology and specialisation-related coursework and requires at least 7.5 EC of social-science research methods. [Official admission requirements](https://www.eur.nl/en/master/social-inequalities-and-change/admission)

| Spring 2027 | Qualitative Research Methodology (GOSSOCI212) · Research Methods & Statistics (BENGATE102) · Social Psychology (HCBPSYC111) · Ethics (GOSPHIL212) |

| Related degree with sociology coursework | GOSSOCI111 establishes sociological concepts. GOSSOCI214 develops class, capital, gender, intersectionality and theoretical analysis. GOSSOCI311 applies civil-society and social theory to empirical questions. |

Comparative politics and public policy connect social inequality to institutions and intervention. Health psychology, brain and behaviour, and social psychology connect the sociology emphasis to mechanisms of stress, identity and group behaviour. The media foundation supports later work on race and environmental representation. Dutch WO degree evidence meets the English rule. Erasmus assesses specialisation fit and requires degree completion by its published deadline.

## MA-08 · Maastricht University · Work and Organisational Psychology

**C · Eligibility cannot be established.** Maastricht's September 2027 route for other academic degrees requires substantial relevance to the specialisation, 27 ECTS of WO-level methods/statistics with empirical research experience, 10 ECTS of academic writing and 24 ECTS of psychology. Applicants must know most topics on a portal checklist. [Official admission requirements](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/education/master/programmes/psychology-work-and-organisational-psychology/admission-requirements)

The complementary business and sociology work addresses organisational context, social theory and applied research. Persuasive communication, stylistics and intervention design build evidence about influence and communication in groups. The dossier includes a motivation statement for non-Psychology graduates, the academic-background form and statistics checklist. English proof is determined through Maastricht's applicant questions. The course plan gives substantive preparation and leaves writing-credit recognition and checklist coverage open.

| Economics or similar degree preparation | Introductory economics progresses to micro, macro, econometrics, public economics and behavioural economics. Accounting, banking and international business help connect models to firms and institutions. Tilburg determines degree equivalence. |
| 12 ECTS of macroeconomics | BENECON211 gives 7.5 EC of focused work in goods, financial and labour markets, business cycles, growth and open-economy policy. BENECON111 has a substantial macro component but also teaches micro and strategic interaction. No subject-credit split is documented. |
| 12 ECTS of microeconomics | BENECON212 gives 7.5 EC of focused work in choice, imperfect competition and general equilibrium. | 6 ECTS of game theory | BENECON111 includes game theory and BENECON212 covers strategic behaviour. The reference supplies no separate 6 EC allocation for formal game theory. Those embedded topics cannot make up a distinct pool by counting the same course credit again. |

The remaining courses connect economic analysis to evidence, policy design, international institutions, market behaviour and business decisions. Tilburg may offer a pre-master up to 30 ECTS. Its committee evaluates the economics scope of the full transcript and course descriptions.

**C · Eligibility cannot be established from the current evidence.** Utrecht's Dutch WO route requires a sufficient bachelor's degree, at least 7.5 EC of statistics and 7.5 EC of programming, with credited R and Python work and specified competencies. Utrecht lists two specific courses from its Applied Data Science minor as an alternative way to establish the foundation. The sample schedule uses UCR courses throughout, so those external minor courses carry no credit here.
