## GRE reasoning matrix

[Official GRE matrix](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/file/gre-admissions-table-sbepdf), page 1.

The column axis is Quantitative Reasoning, 130 through 170 in order. Each row gives the Verbal Reasoning score and the corresponding 41 classifications. `-` is insufficient, `+` is sufficient for board review, and `++` qualifies for automatic admission to International Business if the remaining admission requirements are met. The programme separately requires Analytical Writing of at least 3.5.
