# First production batch review

Reviewed 2026-11-08. The target is September 2027 master’s entry for applicants with a Dutch research-university bachelor’s degree. Coverage includes relevant named and assessed entry routes and the published bridge options for the six pilot targets below. Where the source does not establish the intended master’s intake, the workbook records `unstated`. A published start date for a pre-master establishes that bridge's timing separately.

A second reviewer independently compared the six records with the official admissions sources. The review identified omitted routes, scope restrictions, evidence requirements and application conditions. The revised workbook incorporates these findings and records the remaining uncertainties.

## Retained supporting evidence

### Maastricht Psychology English questionnaire

[Official questionnaire](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/ajax/click-model/CKD63FQ), linked under Language requirements on the 2027–2028 master’s page. Checked 2026-11-08.

- A bachelor’s or master’s taught entirely in English meets the language rule. Upload a letter from the institution or the transcript appendix. International or European Baccalaureate, a US high school diploma or UK GCE A-levels also meet the rule with diploma evidence.
- Maastricht can assess evidence of at least one semester of English-taught degree courses, at least one semester of an internship in an English-speaking workplace, or other professional experience in an English-speaking workplace.
- Otherwise the questionnaire lists IELTS 6.5, TOEFL iBT 90 under its before-2026 label and 4.5 under its after-2026 label, TOEIC listening/reading 720 and speaking/writing 310, Cambridge C1 Advanced grade C or First grade A at 180–184, First B2 grade B at 176–179, or TOEFL paper-delivered reading, listening and writing scores of 20–30 each. Maastricht can request further evidence if in doubt.

### Maastricht Psychology pre-master Dutch questionnaire

[Official questionnaire](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/nl/ajax/click-model/CHHWET4), linked under Taaleisen. Checked 2026-11-08.

Dutch HAVO or VWO and completed Dutch-taught HBO or WO qualify. The questionnaire also lists Flemish secondary education, Walloon secondary school with Dutch as an examination subject, German Abitur with Dutch as an examination or advanced subject, Surinamese VWO or HBO propedeuse, UK GCE A/AS Dutch, French general baccalaureate with Dutch as first, second or third language, European Baccalaureate with Dutch as first or second language, IB Dutch A SL/HL or Dutch B HL, and CNaVT STRT or EDUP including the named older certificates. Applicants outside those branches can submit Dutch State Exam NT2 level II or an equivalent certificate. Maastricht can ask for further evidence.

### Maastricht International Business portfolio and English evidence

[Official portfolio template](https://myapplication-ch.maastrichtuniversity.nl/sites/default/files/2025-08/SBE-Academic-Capabilities-Portfolio-2026-2027.docx), sections 1–3. The programme links this file for September 2027, but the filename gives 2026–2027. Checked 2026-11-08.

- Record the programme and specialisation, previous institution and country, level, degree and field.
- Document quantitative and mathematical courses. Give grades, credits and textbooks, plus a description of at most ten sentences per course.
- Describe in at most 120 words how you recently used verbal or analytical writing skills for an academic task or problem. Include a single-authored English writing sample. Use at most ten pages from longer evidence.
- The form calls for evidence of quantitative, verbal and analytical writing skills. Combine the form and evidence in one PDF for MyApplication. The programme separately offers test routes.

[SBE English questionnaire](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/english-language-requirement-sbe), with [underlying decision tree](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/ajax/click-model/CHHWT6E). Checked 2026-11-08. If proof is requested, a bachelor’s or master’s taught entirely in English qualifies with supporting evidence such as a certified institutional statement. The questionnaire also has a nationality branch and test routes. The International Business page does not establish which applicants with Dutch qualifications are assigned a separate proof task.

The [previously retained Maastricht matrix and qualification lists](2026-11-08-ma-32.md) continue to support the programme’s named routes and GRE option.
