## Tilburg methodology and statistics checklist

[Official checklist](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/sites/default/files/download/Methodology%20Statistics%20Checklist%20TSHD_0_0.pdf), pages 1–2, linked by the national application procedure. Checked 2026-11-08. The applicant reports whether they have no experience, an introduction, or passed coursework for each topic. Where coursework applies, the course name must match the transcript. The form asks for applicant name and date. The university states that previous study of every topic is unnecessary.

- Methodology, items 1–15. Conceptual models, hypothesis types and direction, operational definitions and classification or facet designs, moderation and mediation, confounding and spurious relations, experimental and quasi-experimental designs, within- and between-factor designs, content or corpus analysis, focus groups, interviews, validity, reliability, response scales and sampling methods.
- Basic statistics, items 16–28. Measurement levels, descriptive statistics, graphical summaries, simple linear regression and Pearson correlation, type I and II errors, parametric assumptions, contingency tables and chi-square, independent and paired t-tests, one- and two-way ANOVA, confidence intervals, effect sizes, reliability indices and non-parametric tests.
- Advanced statistics, items 29–36. Multiple and partial correlations, MANOVA and repeated-measures ANOVA, advanced effect sizes, interactions and covariates, diagnostic assumptions, Hayes mediation, logistic regression, principal components and factor analysis.
- Application, items 37–41. Responsible research conduct, defining and recoding data or calculating scores, and practical use of SPSS, R or other statistical software.

## Intake and rule checks

The draft was checked for the distinction between the master intake and separate bridge intakes. Review also checked the 9 proof-based credits within Radboud’s 18-credit analysis pool, Utrecht’s discretionary rather than absolute GPA benchmark, and the difference between Tilburg’s required checklist and an all-topics requirement. Where a committee decides equivalence or assigns a bridge, the workbook records that decision point.
