# Dutch master's admissions and UCR curriculum feasibility

This research pilot tests whether official Dutch WO master's admissions criteria can be collected consistently and used to plan undergraduate preparation at University College Roosevelt. It is separate from Pathways production.

The [pilot protocol](PILOT_PROTOCOL.md) defines the research and validation rules. The August 2026 DUO/RIO higher education export supplies programme identities. Official university sources govern admission. The pilot reads the [existing UCR course reference](../../data/reference/README.md) directly.

Status as of 7 November 2026. Protocol established. The admissions dataset, course assessments, curriculum examples and evaluation are in preparation.
