# Dutch master's admissions and UCR curriculum feasibility

## Pilot results

The pilot covered 24 WO master's programmes at ten universities, with four programmes in each field. The sample includes three research degrees and one double degree. It was selected to test different admissions structures and levels of disciplinary demand. Its proportions describe the sample. The pilot did not use historical admissions decisions or compare its judgments with evaluations of actual UCR transcripts. It therefore establishes feasibility of the research method and leaves predictive accuracy open.

Shares are rounded independently. The pilot found and reviewed official admissions information for all 24 selected routes. It produced 171 requirement records from 33 official sources. Eighteen programmes (75%) published academic criteria specific enough to extract as credit rules, concrete competency lists or named routes. Four combined explicit requirements with important unspecified criteria. Two left the main academic sufficiency judgment broadly discretionary. The course review identified candidate coursework for all 24 programmes. The candidate sets and their formal prerequisites fit within the six covered terms, using at most four courses per term and reserving PPD in year one. That check establishes that the candidate courses can coexist. The six worked examples go further. Each contains 24 distinct courses, totals 180 EC and passed the shared validator for sequencing, offerings and programme rules. Their results include one A, two B and three C cases. ## Admissions evidence

Programme websites generally exposed admissions information through an application page organised by prior education. Several useful criteria appeared one layer deeper, in a faculty matrix, linked PDF, formal regulation or a specialisation section. The national registry identified the recognised programme. It did not provide enough information to establish the operative admissions route. Utrecht's European Governance also shows why study load needs scope. RIO records 60 EC for Utrecht's registered component, while the selected university offering is a two-year double degree. [Programme identities](programmes.csv) · [Official source locations](sources.csv)

Nineteen records preserve at least one material alternative route or condition, such as named-degree entry, a pre-master, a conditional test or a partner route. Applicant category was an essential extraction field throughout. Material route-specific evidence affected at least seven selected cases, including the partner rules in European Governance, the specialisation-specific coursework at Leiden Psychology, the alternative topic combinations in Radboud Cognitive Neuroscience, and the ecology thresholds in Radboud Biology. Some other degrees use shared academic requirements with separate selection or placement for each specialisation.

The most consequential ambiguities were degree equivalence, how much of a mixed course a committee will count, the treatment of course levels, and the credit or duration attached to research and writing. Different numerical conditions can describe different things. Erasmus's UCR-specific 7.00 GPA is an application requirement. Radboud Cognitive Neuroscience describes 7.0 as an illustration of strong performance. The Groningen International Relations faculty and programme pages give different TOEFL thresholds. The pilot retains both sources and identifies the conflict for applicants who need that test. [Requirements and source qualifications](admission_requirements.csv)

## Course evidence and feasibility

UCR could plausibly provide the published academic preparation for ten routes in this sample. Relevant examples include sociology with explicit recognition of liberal arts degrees, interdisciplinary sustainability, and some business and computing routes with specific credit or competency rules. The clearest gaps appeared where a university required a distinct specialist credit pool, a degree-scale sequence or a research milestone of specified duration. Tilburg Economics calls for 6 EC of game theory in a separate pool. Radboud Conservation and Restoration Ecology requires a 10 EC final project with a written thesis. Leiden Biomedical Sciences requires three months of final project research and three weeks of laboratory experience. [Feasibility judgments](feasibility.csv)

The UCR reference supplied official descriptions for all 151 active courses and selected outline evidence for 129. That was enough for most judgments about academic content and progression. The evidence was less conclusive for credit allocation within mixed courses and for activities that have their own duration or assessment conditions. For example, UCR's data courses establish R, Python, SQL and data manipulation, while the reviewed descriptions and outlines leave regular-expression practice unresolved. Maastricht's psychology route allows assessed writing within courses, but the workbook supplies no distinct credit allocation for those activities. [Worked curricula](CURRICULUM_EXAMPLES.md)

The most useful additional evidence would be existing assessed briefs or marked work showing quantitative projects, research and writing scope, formal credit or workload by component where it is recorded, and any separately documented thesis or internship provision. Seek committee decisions for genuinely ambiguous equivalences. A committee decision on one transcript may help interpret similar cases, but its applicability depends on the route, academic year and coursework.

The examples use the last complete fall-entry horizon in the workbook, Fall 2026 through Spring 2029. Master’s criteria recorded now need to be rechecked for that cohort's eventual applications. A new Fall 2027 cohort would need the Spring 2030 offering column before a full schedule can be verified. The project validators establish the Pathways programme rules. An individual admissions decision still depends on the receiving university, the actual transcript and any separate application conditions.

## Operational scalability

The publicly funded DUO/RIO snapshot has 1,788 WO master's rows after excluding rows whose registration or latest intake date precedes the snapshot, grouped into 1,459 provider/registry-unit pairs and 798 provider/recognised-code or parent-code pairs. The 798 figure is an order-of-magnitude planning base. Joint degrees, duplicate provider entries, local variants and route-specific requirements would need to be reconciled for national work.

| Work unit | Planning estimate |
| --- | --- |
| Straightforward programme | 30–60 minutes to locate and extract the operative route, check the source and screen UCR coursework |
| Difficult programme | 1.5–3 hours when several routes, regulations, disputed credit allocations or specialist subjects need review |
| Worked 180 EC example | 2–4 additional hours for allocation, scheduling, validation and an academic review |

These are planning assumptions for a repeatable human-reviewed process. The pilot did not time each operation separately. At roughly 800 programme/provider groups, even the straightforward estimate implies 400–800 hours for first-pass work before difficult routes and detailed examples. A nationwide admissions index with official links is feasible in stages. Maintaining verified subject-level admissions criteria and UCR matches for every national route would require dedicated staff time and subject review.

Automate identification, link checking, source snapshot comparison, extraction drafts, arithmetic, prerequisite checks and schedule validation. Retain human review for the operative route, conflicting regulations, credit and level equivalence, selective requirements and the coherence of curriculum advice. At each academic-year rollover, revisit current programme links and compare the admissions sections or relevant regulations against the last verified text. Reopen changed requirements and their dependent feasibility judgments. Prioritise undated pages, broken links and high-use programmes. A date or wording change can be logged without reopening the entire record if it leaves the criteria intact.

## Student use and next stage

A national search would help students discover programmes and distinguish named-degree, assessed and bridge routes. It should show each route's evidence date and direct link. Its value depends on accurate coverage and preserving applicant-category differences. A curriculum-planning service offers more immediate value for a smaller set of destinations, where an adviser can show the student which courses to take, what assessed work to retain and which questions to take to the receiving institution.

A reverse search could start from the student's transcript and separate plausible destinations, established gaps and decisions that require committee review. It would need course-credit allocation, actual attainment and grade information, the correct entry year and evidence of individual research and writing. The most useful initial cases are explicit rules that can be checked without inferring a degree or a course equivalence from a title.

**Expand in stages.** Start with roughly 40 high-interest programmes across fields and prioritise routes that can yield actionable curriculum advice. Keep the broader national search at the official programme and admissions-link level until verified route coverage and annual maintenance are established. Review uncertain and high-use mappings with receiving admissions teams before exposing any UCR preparation judgments to students. Use the next cohort's complete YearPlan before expanding worked schedules.
