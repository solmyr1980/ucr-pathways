# UCR Pathways — Student Programme Disclaimer

**Status:** The wording below is approved and active in the student app and governing documentation. It supersedes the earlier Etienne wording. The Gerda wording remains recorded separately and is not part of the approved student disclaimer.

## Approved student disclaimer

This app gives you an overview of the courses UCR offers at the time of publication. Because our curriculum keeps developing, the course offer does too: courses may be updated, new ones may be added, and others may be changed or removed. So use this app to explore the possibilities at UCR, but keep in mind that the exact course offer may look different by the time you start. No rights can be derived from the information in this app.

## Additional wording retained for reference

This tool compares the content and structure of bachelor programs. Admission to master’s programs and access to regulated professions always depend on the specific requirements of the relevant program or profession.
