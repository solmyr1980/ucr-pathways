About this programme

A reliable curriculum comparison is currently unavailable. The external curriculum has unresolved requirements; no complete degree pathway is certified here.

African Studies at Leiden examines African histories, societies, languages, literature and arts, and Africa’s connections with the wider world. Students learn Swahili, develop research skills and spend a study period in Africa. The final year includes elective space and an independent thesis. Disciplinary themes are not separate formal specialisations.
