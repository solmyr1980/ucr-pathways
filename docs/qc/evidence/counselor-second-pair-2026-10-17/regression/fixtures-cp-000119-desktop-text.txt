Philosophy, myth & cultural meaning

Philosophy explores meaning, ethics and the self, while literature, myth and heritage examine how cultures express and interpret those questions. Social psychology, sociology, law and political thought connect belief and values to shared public life. This is a broader response rather than a specialist theology or comparative-religion curriculum.
