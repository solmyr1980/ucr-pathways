Qualified academic-core comparison

Nominal external degree: 180 EC. Documented components shown: 165 EC.

Assessed scope: The independently confirmed literary, language, cultural and research study, together with its separate 30-EC open semester and 30-EC restricted Arts minor space: 165 distinct documented EC.

Outside the assessed scope: The unidentified 15 EC of required later replacement/rotation study, any dependent advanced or pedagogical culmination, and certification of a complete ordinary external pathway.

Unknown requirements are not open electives and are not assessed as matched or unmatched components.

German Language and Culture studies German language, literature, linguistics and cultural life, including Dutch–German relations and European contexts. It combines advanced German proficiency, academic inquiry and a required period of study in a German-speaking country.

A broader UCR programme in literary interpretation, language analysis and cultural inquiry meaningfully addresses the confirmed scholarly core, without reproducing German-specialist formation or the unknown later requirements. The unknown required 15 EC are not compared. UCR does not supply advanced German proficiency, the German literary/linguistic sequence, German subject-teaching preparation or the required overseas language experience.

Academic year: 2026–2027

External pathway: Ordinary programme; confirmed academic core

The current regulations repeat three 5-EC modules. The displayed 165 distinct EC are a supported subset of a nominal 180-EC degree; 15 EC of required later study and its replacement/rotation allocation remain unresolved.
