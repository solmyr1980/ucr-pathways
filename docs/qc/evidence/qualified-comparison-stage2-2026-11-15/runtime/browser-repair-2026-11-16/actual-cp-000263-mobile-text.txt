About this programme

UCR currently cannot construct a sufficiently close programme for a meaningful curriculum comparison.

No close UCR comparison

Nominal external degree: 180 EC. Documented components shown: 130 EC.

Assessed scope: The 25 individually supported components totalling 130 EC and their defining architectural/urban-design, technical and research substance.

Outside the assessed scope: The disputed 5/10 EC multidisciplinary project, unreconciled 40/45 EC elective allocation and any certification of a complete external degree pathway. These formal uncertainties do not remove the established UCR architectural-design shortfall.

Unknown requirements are not open electives and are not assessed as matched or unmatched components.

Architecture, Urbanism and Building Sciences studies buildings and urban environments through design, technology, research and social and environmental inquiry. This example follows Architectural Urban Design and Engineering, with a sustained sequence of architectural and urban-design projects.

UCR offers related environmental, spatial and technical study, but its current courses do not sustain a sufficiently close response to the confirmed AUDE architectural and urban-design core. Related UCR environmental, infrastructure, spatial and sustainability courses do not supply the sustained architectural design and building-analysis sequence. The external credit-allocation conflict remains unresolved and is separate from this academic shortfall.

Academic year: 2026–2027

External pathway: Architectural Urban Design and Engineering (AUDE)

The nominal degree is 180 EC. The 25 undisputed components shown total 130 EC. The multidisciplinary project is published as 5 or 10 EC and elective volume as 40 or 45 EC; their allocation remains unreconciled. The omitted 50 EC of the nominal degree are not all unknown required study or open electives: they contain the disputed project and the genuine but unreconciled elective allocation.
