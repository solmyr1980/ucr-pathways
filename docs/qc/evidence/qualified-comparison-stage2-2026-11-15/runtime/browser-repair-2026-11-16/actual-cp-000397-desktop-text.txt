Fiscal Economics studies taxation and its economic effects on governments, firms and households, combining economic analysis with tax law, accounting, finance and ethical judgement. Later study develops specialised fiscal and reporting knowledge and culminates in a seminar and thesis.

A broader UCR programme in economics, public finance and policy meaningfully responds to the confirmed economic-effects and fiscal-policy inquiry, while supplying substantially less statutory-tax and advanced-accounting specialisation. UCR directly studies taxation and redistribution through public economics and supports economic-effects inquiry through microeconomics, macroeconomics, behavioural analysis and empirical methods. Its general legal and accounting courses do not reproduce specialist fiscal-law analysis or advanced reporting.

CLOSEST UCR MATCH
Economics, public finance and policy

A programme examining how economic incentives, public spending and taxation affect households, firms and social welfare. Microeconomics, macroeconomics, behavioural economics and public economics combine with accounting, finance, legal institutions and empirical methods. Policy analysis and ethics evaluate collective choices. This is broader economic-policy study, without the statutory fiscal, advanced-reporting or tax-research specialisation of Fiscal Economics.
