What this comparison shows

A qualified academic comparison of the documented scope of Political Science: National Politics and Governance at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam with 2 illustrative UCR study compositions.

CLOSEST UCR MATCH
Comparative politics and public policy

Study comparative political institutions, constitutional law, European governance and public economics. Investigate policy questions with quantitative and qualitative methods, supported by international relations and historical inquiry. UCR supplies broader comparative and European inquiry, rather than a dedicated Dutch national-institutional sequence or the two VU political research projects.

ADDITIONAL UCR OPTION
Political communication and public debate

A programme studying public argument through rhetoric, stylistics, media interpretation, persuasion, policy writing and AI ethics, anchored in comparative politics, constitutional/EU law and empirical inquiry. Television/media cases retain their actual cultural and American focus; they are not relabelled Dutch political communication or an established VU media specialisation.
