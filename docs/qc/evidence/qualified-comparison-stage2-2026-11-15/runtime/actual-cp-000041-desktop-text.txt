What this comparison shows

A qualified academic comparison of the documented scope of Economics and Business Economics at Radboud University with 2 illustrative UCR study compositions.

CLOSEST UCR MATCH
Economics, business & quantitative policy

A programme linking microeconomics, macroeconomics, public and behavioural economics with econometrics, accounting, finance and business decisions. Climate, governance and political institutions provide specific complements to economic-policy inquiry; specialist economics depth and a required economics thesis are not reproduced.

ADDITIONAL UCR OPTION
Economic policy, responsible business and sustainability

A programme asking how governments and businesses respond to climate pressures and unequal outcomes. It retains economic theory, accounting, statistics and econometrics, while adding environmental governance, political institutions, social inequality and commercial law.
