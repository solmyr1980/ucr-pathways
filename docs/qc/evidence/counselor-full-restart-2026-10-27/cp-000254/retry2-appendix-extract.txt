TER Article 3 Admission
Applicants to the Bachelor Nanobiology must meet the overall requiments of the TU Delft for admission, including the
English language requirements. In addition they must meet programme specific requirements.

TER Article 3.1 Programme specific requirements
Access to education in the Bachelor’s degree programme in Nanobiology is open to individuals possessing a certificate
as stipulated in the law and corresponding ministerial regulations with the proper profile or subject, or anyone who
meets the stated requirements.

The prior education requirements are elaborated below, by type of certificate.

a. Certificate of pre-university education (vwo; as stipulated in Art. 7.24.1 a or b of the WHW) or a Surinamese diploma for pre-
      university education (vwo)
N&T profile          [with Biology]
N&G profile          [with Mathematics B and Physics]
E&M profile          [with Mathematics B, Physics, Chemistry and Biology]
C&M profile          [with Mathematics B, Physics, Chemistry and Biology]
The following applies as well:

     -      individuals possessing a pre-university (vwo) certificate with the S&H profile from before 2007 are admissible;
     -      individuals possessing an ‘old style’ pre-university (vwo) certificate with Mathematics B and Physics, Biology
            and Chemistry in the combination of modules are admissible;
     -      individuals with certificates that do not include the aforementioned modules must rectify these deficiencies
            before they can be registered and admitted (no later than 31 August).

b. Bachelor’s, Master’s or Doctoral degree, a certificate of first-year degree audit for higher professional education or an
     associate degree obtained at a Dutch institution
The following applies to this category:

     -      individuals possessing a pre-university (vwo) certificate, as mentioned in Subsection a, are subject to the
            conditions stated in Subsection a;
     -      individuals possessing certificates from senior general secondary education (havo) or senior secondary
            vocational education (mbo) must rectify the deficiencies relative to the pre-university (vwo) level before they
            can register and be admitted (no later than 31 August).

c. Foreign degree
A foreign degree (regardless of whether it was earned abroad) or diplomas based on a European or International
Baccalaureate programme must be equivalent to the pre-university (vwo) level and contain the subjects equivalent to
Mathematics-B, Physics, Biology and Chemistry. This equivalence is determined by the Executive Board.

Individuals in this category must also meet the requirements of satisfactory linguistic mastery of English as stated in
the TER.

d. University Entrance Exam
1.       The process of conducting the admissions examination, as specified in Article 7.29 Sections 2 and 3 of the WHW,
         is assigned to the TU Delft University Entrance Examination Committee established for the joint programmes.

2.       Individuals who have reached the age of 21 years and who would like to be eligible for a university entrance
         examination must possess the following:

     -      a partial pre-university education (vwo) certificate for the courses [Mathematics-B, Biology, Chemistry and
            Physics], or a certificate from a continuing education course or a test administered by the institution, and

3.       The TU Delft University Entrance Examination Committee assesses whether the candidate possesses the

TER Article 3.2 Admission process for Nanobiology
Admission to the programme is granted to those who meet the conditions set forth in the Act and the specific prior
education requirements and the requirements regarding sufficient proficiency of English languages described in TER
article 3.1 above. Admission to the programme also requires participating in the programme specific Mandatory
Matching module which is subject to additional requirements. This applies to applicants in 2026-2027 applying for
admission for academic year 2027-2028.

TER Article 5 Goal of the programme
1.       The programme is intended to educate students to earn a Bachelor of Science degree in Nanobiology, providing
         them knowledge, insight and skills in the area of Nanobiology,

Graduates will be eligible for a follow-up programme at the Master’s level, in any case, the Master’s degree programmes
in Nanobiology at TU Delft/Erasmus University.

2.       Graduates must meet the final attainment levels (FAL) for the degree programme. The Bachelor of Science
         Nanobiology programme has the aim to bridge the fields of mathematics, physics and biology. The graduate:

         FAL.1.   Understand fundamental knowledge of Math, Physics and Biology in field of Nanobiology including
                  reading scientific literature (knowledge)
         FAL.2.   Use methods and techniques of Nanobiology research including computational biosciences and imaging
                  (Methods and Techniques)
         FAL.3.   Conduct experiments in fields of molecular biology, biophysics, computational biosciences, imaging,
                  including troubleshooting (Practicals)
         FAL.4.   Design research questions and experiments (Design)
         FAL.5.   Collaborate effectively in international and interdisciplinary environments (Interdisciplinary)
         FAL.6.   Communicate scientific ideas and results in oral, visual and written forms (Communicate)
         FAL.7.   Critically discuss ethical concepts in the field of nanobiological research and relevance to society (Ethics)




                                     Paragraph 2 Structure Overview
Article 1 Division of the academic year
The academic year of the programme is divided into four quarters following the TU Delft Academic Calendar. The
schedule is published in the TU Delft Timetable, with updates provided by teachers during the course via Brightspace.

Article 1.1 Year 1 and 2
In every quarter, education is scheduled for 8 weeks followed by a study week and then an exam week. During the
study week new material is not provided, however Q&A sessions, practical make up sessions, and exam practice
sessions may be scheduled. Exigent circumstances may require scheduling during this time.

Article 5 Third year
Year 3 consists of 30 EC of a minor, 10 EC of electives and a 20 EC bachelor end project.

Article 5.1 Minor and Minors Abroad
Nanobiology students may choose any available thematic minor offered by TU Delft, Leiden University or Erasmus
University. Students receiving fewer than 30 EC for their minor must take additional courses to fulfil the requirement
which turns it into a self-composed minor.

Students may choose to compose their own minor though this will need approval of the Board of Examiners. This can
be a minor from another Dutch university, a 15 EC minor plus courses, or exchange minor.

Students wishing to do a minor outside of the Netherlands (exchange minor) who have not passed all first year courses
and all courses of the first semester of second year should know that doing an exchange minor may result in study
delay and should discuss their study plan with the academic counsellor.

Article 5.2. Electives
Students may freely choose 10 EC of electives from the list below. Any other electives must be approved by the Board
of Examiners prior to taking the course. Students must enroll in the course in Brightspace before the course begins.
Electives may have limited enrolment, or prerequisites which are detailed in the study guide. Students wishing to take
a course outside of Nanobiology as an elective can request this via the Board of Examiners. This should be approved
before starting the course.
