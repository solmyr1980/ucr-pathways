Article 11 (Compulsory) participation in the degree programme

Art. 7.13 paragraph 2 sub t, WHW - FSC right of approval, BoS advisory powers

    1. All students are expected to participate actively in the degree programme for which they are registered.

    2. Where necessary, participation in practical exercises is compulsory for admission to the relevant exam. The
       Board of Examiners is authorised to grant an exemption from that obligation, whether or not subject to
       alternative requirements.

    3. Any additional obligations are described for each component in the course description in the Study Guide.

Article 12 Programme evaluation

Art. 7.13 paragraph 2 sub a1, WHW - BoS right of approval

    1. The Director of Studies shall ensure the evaluation of the education.

    2. The manner in which the education in the degree programme is evaluated shall be laid down in the faculty’s
       Quality Assurance Manual ‘Onderwijskwaliteitszorg 2012’, which is submitted to the Faculty Student Council
       and the Board of Studies.

    3. The Director of Studies shall inform the Board of Studies of the outcomes of the evaluation, the intended
       adjustments in response thereto, and the effect of the actual adjustments.

Paragraph 4 Registration and deregistration for courses and examinations


Article 12a Mandatory course registration

ESC right of approval, BoS advisory powers

Students of the Nanobiology (BSc joint degree) degree programme don't have to register for the courses which are
part of the examination programme. If registration is required for a specific course or programme component – for
example due to limited capacity or because of the planning of the number of groups and rooms – this is stated in the
study guide, including details about the registration method and deadlines.
