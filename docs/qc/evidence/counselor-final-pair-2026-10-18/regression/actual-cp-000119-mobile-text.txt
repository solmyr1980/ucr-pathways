About this programme

UCR currently cannot construct a sufficiently close programme for a meaningful curriculum comparison.

Theology and Religious Studies at VU Amsterdam examines religious traditions, beliefs, texts and practices, and their place in society. The curriculum shown uses the 2026–2027 Religion and Worldviews route, which combines study of several traditions with philosophy, social perspectives and research. The selected offering is part-time.

UCR offers individual courses on myth, philosophy and religious art or heritage. These provide useful related perspectives, but the available courses do not sustain a programme centred on religious traditions, sacred texts and lived religion. We therefore show VU’s complete curriculum without presenting a UCR programme as a comparable alternative.

Academic year: 2026–2027

External pathway: Religion and Worldviews; Dutch part-time offering

The 180-EC VU curriculum is paced over six calendar years. Teaching is mainly Dutch in the first two academic stages and English in the selected third stage. The newer route advertised for 2027 is not substituted here.
