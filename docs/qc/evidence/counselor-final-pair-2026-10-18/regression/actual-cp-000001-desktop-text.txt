Public Administration (Bestuurskunde) at Erasmus University Rotterdam examines how governments and public organizations address social problems. Students combine policy, politics, law, sociology, economics and management with social research, a public-sector internship and a bachelor project. The selected programme is the Dutch-language full-time bachelor.

The first UCR option combines the main neighbouring disciplines; the second adds a substantial climate and sustainability focus. Neither reproduces EUR’s dedicated public-administration degree. In particular, general public policy, policy analysis, the required internship and the bachelor-project sequence remain important differences. Shared rows show content relationships, including partial matches, rather than course equivalence.

Academic year: 2026–2027

External pathway: Dutch-language Public Administration (Bestuurskunde); full-time

The complete EUR curriculum comprises 180 EC over three years, including a 15-EC approved minor. Some lectures are in English; the Dutch offering retains Dutch working groups, assignments and assessments.

CLOSEST UCR MATCH
Closest match to Bestuurskunde

Politics, law, sociology and economics provide different ways to examine public institutions and policy choices. Research methods, ethics, organizational psychology and leadership connect those perspectives to public problems. This is an interdisciplinary UCR response, with gaps in dedicated public-administration teaching and compulsory professional practice.

ADDITIONAL UCR OPTION
Climate & sustainability governance

Public institutions and policy are studied alongside climate change, water management, environmental justice and resource stewardship. Science and laboratory study support informed analysis of sustainability choices. The programme combines governance with environmental knowledge; it is not a specialist public-administration or environmental-engineering degree.
