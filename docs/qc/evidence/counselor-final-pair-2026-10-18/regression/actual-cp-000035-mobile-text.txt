Biology at Radboud University studies living systems from plants, animals and ecosystems to cells, genes, the brain and human health. A broad foundation is followed by domain choices, an essay and a research-department internship. The Free domain allows a broad profile across biology and medical biology.

The UCR options pursue biological questions through three different combinations: broad biology, ecology and environmental study, or human biology with neuroscience and health psychology. Shared rows indicate important partial content, not equivalent courses or qualifications. Specialist gaps and the different balance of each option remain important. The Radboud curriculum shown is its provisional prospective overview and may change.

Academic year: 2027–2028

External pathway: Free domain

Official prospective overview, subject to change. The 180 EC includes 66 EC of domain and elective choices, subject to programme requirements. The introductory programme and Research Skills are additional compulsory non-credit obligations.

What this comparison shows

A curriculum-level comparison of Biology at Radboud University with 3 illustrative UCR study compositions.

CLOSEST UCR MATCH
Biology across organisms, ecosystems & health

Study living systems from biodiversity, evolution and ecosystems to cells, biochemistry, human physiology, genetics and disease. Laboratory work, statistics and data analysis support these biological questions; environmental science, sustainability governance and ethics add complementary perspectives.

ADDITIONAL UCR OPTION
Ecology, climate & environmental biology

Study biodiversity, evolution, ecosystems and conservation alongside climate change and environmental systems. Spatial analysis, soils, biogeochemistry and sustainability research connect ecological questions with environmental assessment; governance, justice and resource geoscience provide complementary approaches.

ADDITIONAL UCR OPTION
Human biology, neuroscience & health psychology

Study molecular and cellular biology, human physiology, infection, genetics and disease together with neurobiology and cognition. A substantial psychology strand examines behaviour, mental health, development and assessment. Laboratory work, epidemiology, statistics and research synthesis support this combination; it is not a specialist medical or clinical qualification.

Important limitation

This is a curriculum comparison. A UCR composition is not the same disciplinary degree and does not by itself establish a professional qualification or automatic eligibility for a particular master’s programme.

Rows show curricular relationships, including partial matches, rather than course equivalence. A blank cell means there is no closely comparable component in that row. Optional independent research is additional to the displayed 180 EC.
