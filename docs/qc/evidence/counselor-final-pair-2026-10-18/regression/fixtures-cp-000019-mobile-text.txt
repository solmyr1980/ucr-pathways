Arts and Culture Studies at Erasmus University Rotterdam examines how arts and creative industries work in society. It combines cultural sociology, economics, management, media and heritage with research methods. Students develop a focus area, complete an arts-and-culture internship and undertake an individual graduation project.

The three UCR programmes offer different combinations of relevant fields. Matches show important shared content rather than course equivalence; blank cells preserve substantive differences. None of these programmes is an arts-and-health pathway. UCR’s general sociology and organisational study do not reproduce IBACS’s specialist cultural-sector curriculum or mandatory placement.

Academic year: 2026–2027

External pathway: Culture and Economics focus; minor pathway
What this comparison shows

A curriculum-level comparison of International Bachelor Arts and Culture Studies at Erasmus University Rotterdam with 3 illustrative UCR study compositions.

CLOSEST UCR MATCH
Arts, culture, economy & society

Art history, heritage and literature connect with media, sociology, economics and organisations. Research methods support the study of cultural works, markets and social contexts; these general disciplines complement the arts without reproducing IBACS’s specialist cultural-sector sequence.

ADDITIONAL UCR OPTION
Culture, society & public life

Art, heritage and history connect with sociology, law, philosophy and public policy to explore inequality, collective life and social conflict. Qualitative and quantitative methods support inquiry into culture’s place in public life; this is not an arts-and-health pathway.

ADDITIONAL UCR OPTION
Culture, media & digital society

Film, media and literature connect with rhetoric, communication, audience research and digital change. Sociology and data methods support the study of representation, platforms and cultural participation; the programme does not provide a specialist cultural-sociology sequence.

Important limitation

This is a curriculum comparison. A UCR composition is not the same disciplinary degree and does not by itself establish a professional qualification or automatic eligibility for a particular master’s programme.

Rows show curricular relationships, including partial matches, rather than course equivalence. A blank cell means there is no closely comparable component in that row. Optional independent research is additional to the displayed 180 EC.
