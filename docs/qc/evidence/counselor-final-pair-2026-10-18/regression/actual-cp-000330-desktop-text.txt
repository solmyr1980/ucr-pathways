About this programme

UCR currently cannot construct a sufficiently close programme for a meaningful curriculum comparison.

Mechanical Engineering at the University of Twente studies how physical machines, structures and energy systems work and how to design and improve them. Mathematics, mechanics, thermodynamics, materials and manufacturing are applied in successive engineering projects. The Enschede curriculum includes 30 EC of minor choices and a final research assignment with a separate reflection on its societal implications.

UCR offers relevant study in product design, robot computation, mathematics and sustainable technology. These connections do not provide a sufficiently sustained programme in analysing and designing physical mechanical systems. The mechanics, heat and fluid science, materials and manufacturing integrated through Twente’s engineering projects remain substantial gaps. We therefore show Twente’s complete curriculum without presenting a UCR programme as a comparable alternative.

Academic year: 2026–2027

External pathway: Enschede; standard curriculum with minor choices

The two 15-EC minor positions preserve approved choice; no particular minor is assumed. The final assignment comprises 12 EC of research and 3 EC on societal implications.
