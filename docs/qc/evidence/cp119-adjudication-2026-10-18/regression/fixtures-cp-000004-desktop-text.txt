What this comparison shows

A curriculum-level comparison of Bachelor of Psychology at Erasmus University Rotterdam with 3 illustrative UCR study compositions.

CLOSEST UCR MATCH
Closest match to Psychology

A broad closest feasible psychology programme combining social, developmental, clinical, cognitive and organisational psychology with statistics, research methods, assessment-related study and cross-disciplinary perspectives on behaviour.

ADDITIONAL UCR OPTION
Brain & cognition

A Brain and Cognition route centred on cognitive neuroscience, perception, higher-order functions, language, biological foundations and quantitative/data-oriented research.

ADDITIONAL UCR OPTION
Psychology, leadership & organisations

A Work and Organisational Psychology route centred on organisational behaviour, leadership, diversity, negotiation, workplace wellbeing, behavioural decision-making and applied organisational research.

Important limitation

Rows show curricular relationships, including partial matches, rather than course equivalence. A blank cell means there is no closely comparable component in that row. Optional independent research is additional to the displayed 180 EC.
