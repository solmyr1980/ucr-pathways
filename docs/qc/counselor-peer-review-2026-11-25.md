# Consistency of counselor academic decisions, 25 November 2026

The review covered all 39 academic no-matches with an accepted comparison for the same national programme code or an equivalent title. Eighteen now have a UCR comparison. Twenty-one retain their no-match decision. The peer check also withdrew four previously accepted comparisons. Each count refers to a provider record. Paired records can describe the same curriculum.

| Canonical decision | At screening | After review |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| At least one UCR comparison | 345 | 359 |
| Academic no-match | 77 | 63 |
| External programme unresolved | 15 | 15 |
| Registry exception | 1 | 1 |
| Total | 438 | 438 |

The initial screen found 31 cases by national code and eight more by equivalent English or Dutch titles. The other 38 academic no-matches did not meet either screen. The review establishes the extent of the problem in the selected group. It cannot estimate how often different thresholds affected programmes with other names or codes. [Screening baseline](https://github.com/solmyr1980/ucr-pathways/tree/763d434690aaf043159f094f81b98104a4951654)

For each family, the review compared the exact route, required subjects, advanced work and scope of the accepted peer with the strongest documented UCR course combination. A broader pathway qualifies when UCR courses develop a sustained question or subject that matters to the external programme. Methods alone can be insufficient even when the schedule is feasible. Open elective space and an optional research topic supply no assumed subject matter. The source for each external curriculum is its production decision and cited university evidence. The UCR source is the enriched course catalogue. The exact changed files and their preceding academic versions are recorded in the [decision manifest](evidence/counselor-peer-review-2026-11-25.json).

The original Applied Physics cases serve as a check on the standard. Groningen CP214 retains three comparisons and Eindhoven CP273 retains two. Both teach modelling, control or measurement within the verified external programme. The introduction qualifies the considerable differences in disciplinary depth. Delft CP258 has an unresolved external curriculum and was outside the academic no-match screen. Its verified first year accounts for 60 EC.
