# Deferred counselor comparisons: materiality pilot and policy recommendation

15 November 2026 · Analytical report only · `solmyr1980/ucr-pathways`, `main`

## Decision in brief

Adopt a **modified, narrowly bounded materiality standard**, subject to a separate implementation decision. The pilot supports six bounded qualifications, including one for a partly unpublished curriculum. It also supports two academic no-match recommendations and two continuing deferrals. None meets the current complete-pathway standard, and none is approved for production by this report.

The proposed change concerns what must be known about the **external pathway**, not the academic strength required of a UCR alternative. A qualification can accommodate an unresolved external credit allocation or access condition when the comparison remains defensible under the credible alternatives. It cannot turn a coherent but weakly related UCR program into a meaningful response. The clearest example is Tax Law: setting aside its 3-EC registration conflict does not supply missing fiscal-law teaching at UCR.

| Hypothetical disposition | Cases | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Verified comparison under current rules | None | 0 |
| Qualified comparison | CP250, CP060, CP033, CP440, CP317, CP266 | 6 |
| No defensible UCR match | CP396, CP277 | 2 |
| Unresolved | CP099, CP072 | 2 |

These are case-level analytical recommendations, not six completed production packages. The recommendation for CP060 uses a temporary repair to its closest UCR trial; CP250 includes a new feasibility demonstration. Additional alternatives are not automatically accepted. CP317 is the boundary case for whether a comparison may deliberately leave a substantial, academically consequential part of the external degree unrepresented. Its qualification is defensible only for the narrower claim specified below. If that product scope is declined, the counts become five qualified comparisons, two no-matches and three unresolved cases.

**The 367 previously verified dispositions are preserved.** The ten pilot cases remain operationally deferred, and the other 61 have received no new individual academic disposition here. No case, audit register, governing rule, application, schedule, model setting, discovery index or deployment was changed.

## Proposed operational acceptance standard

### Separate the conclusion from the completeness of its evidence

Record two different questions: **what academic conclusion is justified**, and **how completely the external pathway has been verified**. A no-match may be secure despite an incomplete allocation. Conversely, an external curriculum can be numerically complete while its UCR alternative remains academically weak.

| Conclusion | Required threshold |
|---|---|
| Verified comparison | All current academic, complete-pathway, UCR, comparison, record and final-review gates pass. |
| Qualified comparison | The exact target and neutral cohort/route are established; defining subjects and methods are supported; a meaningful, fully feasible UCR closest response exists; every remaining uncertainty is bounded; the permitted academic claim survives each credible adverse resolution; presentation accurately limits that claim. |
| No defensible UCR match | A genuine strongest-response attempt against current UCR content establishes a substantive shortfall in the target's defining inquiry. The known external core already establishes that shortfall, and the unresolved facts could not remove it. A missing degree title, language sequence or professional license alone is insufficient. |
| Unresolved | A credible resolution could change the neutral route, defining emphasis, strongest UCR response, required preparation, substantive judgment or a reader's interpretation beyond what the proposed qualification can honestly contain. Also use this outcome if the uncertainty cannot be bounded from evidence. |

### Apply a claim-specific counterfactual test

For each unknown, record: the precise fact; the applicable source/cohort; what is known independently; credible alternative resolutions; the effect of each on the proposed academic claim; and the presentation consequence. Do this separately for curriculum content, allocation and access. Do not describe all prerequisites as administrative, or all thesis uncertainty as academically material.

A positive qualification must satisfy all of the following:

1. **Stable academic basis.** Official evidence establishes the program's defining inquiry, central subjects/methods and relevant choices. Cohorts are not blended. Route selection remains neutral and cannot be chosen for UCR convenience.
2. **Unchanged academic threshold.** The closest UCR response pursues that inquiry through actual course content. General research methods, context and freely chosen project possibilities cannot stand in for missing taught subject matter. Every non-PPD course has a specific direct or complementary role.
3. **Independent UCR feasibility.** The complete UCR program passes all current 24-course, 180-EC, six-term, advanced-course, PPD, prerequisite and offering rules. Any extra alternative separately passes substantive distinctness, the four-course/30-EC floor and the stopping rule.
4. **Bounded uncertainty and robust claim.** Each credible adverse resolution leaves the stated comparison defensible. If the unknown would change a detailed relationship, that relationship must be withheld; if it would change the program-level judgment, qualification fails. A qualification may narrow what is compared, but cannot silently change the external target or substitute a hypothetical applicant's interests.
5. **Honest representation.** Preserve exact known components and credits once. Keep conflicting values in internal evidence; display neither as settled. Identify unknown requirements separately from open elective space. An unidentified amount calculated by subtraction is a diagnostic remainder, not a verified course or elective entitlement.
6. **Reviewed limitation and approval.** State what the comparison establishes, what remains unknown and the educational differences that matter. Require a separately identified source-first challenge and academic, mechanical and content checks before any later production approval. Recheck the conclusion when relevant new evidence arrives.

There is **no automatic EC allowance, percentage-verified threshold or number-of-matches quota**. CP250's 1-EC difference, CP060's duplicated 5 EC, CP266's aggregate 5-EC conflict and CP317's unpublished 30 EC are different kinds of uncertainty. The amount is relevant to possible consequences, not a decision rule by itself. Qualification is about the robustness of an explicitly limited claim, not about declaring an unresolved fact harmless in every context.

Failure conditions include an uncertain target/route; importing outgoing-cohort rules; inventing a substitute, waiver or credit weight; portraying all possible choices as compulsory; reliance on a UCR student's unverified freedom to create missing specialist teaching; or a limitation that reveals that the alternative has abandoned the target's defining academic object. Optional independent research outside the scheduled 180 EC cannot repair any of these failures.

### CP250 — Clinical Technology

**Established facts and unknown.** The controlling [2026–27 BSc OER][s250] requires 180 EC in Article 7, yet Appendix 3 assigns KT2755 4.5 EC. The exact current [bachelor-linked course entry][s250course] assigns 3.5 EC. The [latest source review][e250] records all 26 taught components: 25 weights agree, and the catalogue gives 60/60/30 taught EC plus the required 30-EC minor. This is now contrary evidence within the bachelor, not merely a master-bridge inference. No adopted correction was established. The active canonical conservatively preserves the first 60 EC; that does not mean the rest of the academic identity is unknown.

**Academic test.** The strongest UCR response combines anatomy, physiology, disease mechanisms, pharmacological modeling and laboratory science with calculus, signals/systems, programming, imaging and computational methods. Appendix A supplies a newly tested 24-course demonstration. Anatomy uses medical images; pharmacology uses quantitative drug-response models; disease study uses clinical cases. These provide substantive biomedical anchors. General computer vision, robotics and consumer-product design remain methods or complementary engineering contributions, not documented clinical applications.

The strongest objection is that an assembled biomedical/computational program does not reproduce the external degree's integration of engineering, patient care, medical physics and safety. This is a real limitation. It does not eliminate the substantial biomedical and technical inquiry present in the UCR composition, but it limits the permitted label and claims. Resolving the placement to 4.5 EC, or requiring a corresponding administrative adjustment elsewhere, would not reverse that narrower judgment. A no-match based only on absent clinical licensing or exact device training would disregard substantial taught content; a claim to equivalent clinical-technology preparation would be indefensible.

**Recommendation and exact boundary.** Qualified comparison, moderate confidence. Permit an academic comparison of biomedical science with quantitative/computational methods. Leave the placement weight disputed; assume neither that 3.5 EC controls nor that a correction has been adopted. Do not fabricate a one-credit offset, medical-device studio, clinical robotics assignment, patient placement or professional access.

Draft limitation: “UCR can combine biomedical science with mathematical and computational methods. It does not reproduce this degree's integrated clinical and medical-engineering training or patient placements. Official sources disagree about the placement's credit weight, so the external degree is not presented as a fully reconciled 180-EC pathway.”

An official KT2755 correction is indispensable for a verified pathway, but not for this limited academic comparison. It may become available through an amended catalogue/regulation; its timing is unknown. Preserve both source versions, corrected interest evidence and all 16 prior course leads. A later production package needs fresh traceability, alignment, candidate/stopping and content review for the new trial; the trial itself is not that package. Use the common qualified/partial representation proposed below, rather than changing a credit to satisfy today's compiler.
