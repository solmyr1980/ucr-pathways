# Stage 1: bounded materiality implementation and production results

15 November 2026 · `solmyr1980/ucr-pathways`, `main` · Stage 2 and release remain unauthorized

## Decision in brief

Stage 1 implements qualified academic comparisons and robust academic no-matches without relaxing UCR course, programme or feasibility requirements. The ten production reviews yield **six qualified comparisons, two robust no-matches and two continuing deferrals**. The six positive cases contain **12 accepted UCR programmes**. CP317 is approved only as an explicitly limited academic-core comparison.

The active audit now contains **375 approved academic dispositions and 63 deferrals**: 311 approved comparisons, 63 approved no-matches and one registry exception. The 63 deferrals comprise CP099, CP072 and the other 61 cases outside Stage 1. All 367 original approvals and all 61 other deferrals retain byte-identical decisions, canonicals and detailed case evidence. Their academic judgments were not reopened.

The final case counts agree with the analytical pilot, but production approval required new contracts, individual source-first challenges, corrected records and completed validations. The production review also established a second CP266 alternative after a stronger materials-and-energy candidate was tested. The pilot's temporary trials and proposed dispositions were not treated as approvals.

**Recommendation:** proceed to Stage 2 in small manual batches under this standard. Retain cases whose uncertainty can still change the permitted academic conclusion. Deployment, automations, final discovery products and public release require separate authority.

## Implemented acceptance contract

The [Production Instructions §2.1A](../UCR_Pathways_Production_Instructions.md) own the operative standard. The [Master Specification](../UCR_Pathways_Master_Specification.md) owns its product meaning; the [review protocol](counselor-comprehensive-reaudit-protocol.md) owns its execution and audit trail.

| Route | Academic requirement | External representation and approval |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary verified comparison | Existing target, academic, alternative, course and feasibility gates | Complete coherent external 180 EC remains the default. Existing records require no migration. |
| Qualified comparison | Exact target/cohort and neutral route; verified defining substance; strongest meaningful UCR response; each credible adverse resolution preserves the explicitly limited claim | Explicit validated acceptance evidence, reviewed partial status and prominent assessed/excluded scope. All UCR programmes remain complete 24-course/180-EC compositions. |
| Qualified academic core | The confirmed core supports a meaningful response although unknown later training prevents a full-degree claim | Name the confirmed core and genuine open space; exclude unknown required study and culmination from both relationships and numeric alignment. |
| Robust no-match | Verified defining substance and the strongest current UCR response establish a substantive shortfall; missing external facts cannot reverse it | Existing no-match exception type, with explicit negative proof and a truthful partial external view when needed. |
| Unresolved | A credible resolution could change the target, applicable route, defining weight, strongest response or permitted academic conclusion; or the boundary cannot be established | Retain the unresolved exception and external-only display. Prose and presentation flags cannot confer qualification. |

There is no EC allowance, percentage-verified threshold, match quota or public score. An uncertainty can be material to formal progression but containable within an academic claim. It can also prevent a full-degree comparison while permitting a narrower core claim. These judgments require explicit adverse-resolution tests, not the description of every missing fact as “administrative.”

The optional internal `acceptance` object records the standard, basis, scope, claim, target context, verified component IDs, external/UCR/challenge evidence references, review method/date and each uncertainty with credible adverse resolutions. Its two bases are `qualified-comparison` and `robust-no-match`. Ordinary verified records continue through the existing complete-pathway gates without this object. Top-level `comparison` and `exception` meanings and schema version 2.0 are preserved.

Audience content adds `comparisonScope`: a readable label, assessed scope, excluded scope and nominal external credits. Accepted bounded records also require programme/status explanations and a reviewed partial curriculum. A documented total of 180 EC does not certify unresolved access conditions. Disputed weights are omitted from the summed verified boundary; their conflicting values remain in internal evidence. Unknown requirements never become electives, balancing credits, matched cells or fictitious theses.

Every included UCR programme still requires 24 unique courses, 180 EC, four courses in each of six terms, Year-1 PPD, at least six advanced courses, actual earlier prerequisites and actual planned offerings. Course-to-concept relevance, qualitative distinctness, the pairwise 30-EC floor and sequential stopping remain independent requirements. Optional independent research remains additional to the scheduled 180 EC.

### What the difficult cases establish

CP250 and CP266 require more than prominent caveats. Their accepted programmes contain actual biomedical or product/user inquiry and substantive technical subjects. Generic computing alone would not suffice. CP266's initially rejected circular-business trial did not settle its best additional possibility: actual materials laboratories, resource recovery and energy-system teaching supported a stronger, genuinely different second programme. Health/wellbeing and aesthetic-interaction leads were considered separately; neither supplied a sufficiently evidenced further taught response.

CP060 and CP277 expose a different boundary. The Dutch case sustains literary interpretation and language analysis across its alternatives while making the national-specialist gap central. In the African case, one substantial regional art/heritage course and specific comparative connections do not establish sustained Africa-centred inquiry throughout a complete programme. This is a programme-level judgment, not a matching-course quota or a rejection based on language/travel alone.

CP317's unknown final study is **material to a full-degree comparison**. Approval does not declare it harmless. The permitted claim survives because it explicitly excludes that study and compares only a separately confirmed core. CP099 lacks enough established later disciplinary development for the same claim. CP072's repeated/rotating arrangements do not establish a comparably stable applicable core. Neither can be admitted merely because a partial credit total is large.

## Remaining limitations and Stage 2 decision

CP099 still needs the revised second-year credit/choice table and final research/preparation/communication allocation and entry rules. CP072 still needs an applicable rotation/replacement allocation, mobility-substitution rules and approved fulfilment of the required research seminar. Their completed Stage 1 reviews preserve these specific blockers; passing technical checks does not approve them.

The eight newly approved cases retain the external uncertainties stated in their acceptance evidence. Resolving an uncertainty may permit removal of a qualification, but no future clarification or timetable is assumed. Borderline academic judgments, especially CP250, CP060, CP266 and CP277, remain judgments from an explicitly identified same-AI review. They have source-based challenges and bounded claims, not independent human endorsement.

Stage 1 supports proceeding to the **other 61 deferrals** in small manual batches, preferably grouped by reusable source families while finishing each case individually. Reuse sound source/course evidence by version; test each case's actual adverse resolutions and strongest UCR response. Do not extrapolate approval rates from the pilot or turn one family's administrative uncertainty into another case's academic conclusion. Keep the existing workers exhausted and final release separate.

**Decision requested:** authorize Stage 2 for the other 61 deferrals under this implemented standard, in small manual batches, while retaining material uncertainties as deferrals. This would not authorize deployment, automation changes or final release. CP099 and CP072 should be reconsidered only when their specified evidence or the authorized scope changes.
