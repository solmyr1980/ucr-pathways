# UCR Pathways video assets

High-resolution PNG exports of the full UCR curriculum butterfly and the individual course clusters. SVG exports may also be used if available.

These images are inputs to the experimental curriculum-video sub-project, whose agreed concept, storyboard, narration, source records, technical tests and continuation instructions are documented in [the pilot production brief](../../pilot/curriculum-video/README.md).

Current assets:

- `curriculum.png` — complete butterfly
- `BEN.png` — Business & Entrepreneurship
- `DIS.png` — Data Science & Intelligent Systems
- `ENS.png` — Environment & Sustainability
- `GOS.png` — Government & Society
- `HCB.png` — Human Health & Well-being
- `MCC.png` — Media, Culture & Communication

The PNGs are source visuals, not rendered videos. Prototype MP4s are not currently versioned in this directory.
